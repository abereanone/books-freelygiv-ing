title: About the Author
---

Michael is a particular Baptist preacher who has served Christ’s Church as pastor, evangelist, teacher, writer, and application developer. He lives in Ohio with his wife, Erin, and their two youngest boys, and is active in abortion abolition work and the local church. One of his favorite verses is Galatians 2:20: “I have been crucified with Christ, and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself up for me.”

You can find more of his work at michaelcoughlin.net and thingsabove.us.
