John Flavel (1627–1691) wrote this treatise in 1667 for his flock at Dartmouth, Devon, while the Act of Uniformity kept him from them. This text follows the American Tract Society edition (New York), which was reprinted from the Glasgow edition of 1830. Scanning errors have been corrected, a few spellings modernized, and footnotes and a glossary added for old words and old meanings.

Scripture quotations in the text are from the King James Version, as Flavel quoted it. Verses he cites without quoting are printed in the footnotes from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), with the divine name printed as “Yahweh,” and where the BSB’s “you” or “your” is plural, it is marked “you+” or “your+.” Both are in the public domain.
