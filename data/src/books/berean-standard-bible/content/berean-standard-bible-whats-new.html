Berean Standard Bible: what is different in this edition

Yahweh

Where the BSB prints Lord or God in small capitals, standing for God’s name in the Hebrew, this edition prints the name itself: Yahweh. That is 5,844 verses.

Psalm 23:1 — Yahweh is my shepherd; I shall not want.

You+

English “you” can mean one person or many. Where the Hebrew or Greek is plural, this edition marks it “you+,” “your+” or “yours+.” That is 3,546 verses.

Matthew 5:13 — You+ are the salt of the earth.
