This do, and the Lord accept thee; remember all thy offering, and accept all thy sacrifices; the Lord hear thee in the day of thy trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee: the Lord grant thee according to thine own heart, and fulfill all thy counsel: the Lord remember thee with the favor he beareth unto his children, and visit thee with his salvation.
