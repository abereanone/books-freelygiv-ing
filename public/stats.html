Meditations on Psalm 119 had the most activity: 92 views of its page, 18 opened to read online and about 135 whole copies downloaded. Across all books, people opened a book to read online about 146 times and downloaded about 1,070 whole copies. Of the copies, 79% were PDF, 11% print files and 8% EPUB.

"Read online" counts each time someone opened a book's online version on this site. The copies columns are whole copies: all the data sent for that file divided by its size, because a browser often fetches a large PDF in pieces. Someone who opens a book again counts again, and someone who reads only a few pages counts as part of a copy. "Print zips" are the files for printing a paper copy. A few files' sizes could not be checked; those count each request as a copy.
